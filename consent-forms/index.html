SMS consent forms

These are the paper forms MPMS, Inc. uses to collect written consent for the MPMS Operations Alerts SMS program. Consent is collected offline during onboarding — vendors and contractors sign the first form, employees the second. There is no web sign-up form.

The consent wording on both forms is reproduced verbatim from section 2 of the SMS Messaging Terms & Conditions.

This page is published so the forms can be inspected without a file-sharing link. It is not part of the site navigation.
