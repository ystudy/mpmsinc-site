MPMS, Inc. does not send marketing or promotional text messages, and does not send text messages to the general public.

1. Program description

MPMS, Inc. ("MPMS", "we", "us", "our") operates an SMS text message program called MPMS Operations Alerts. The program is used strictly to conduct our property management business with two audiences:

  • Employees of MPMS, Inc. — office and field maintenance personnel.
  • Vendors and contractors — trade contractors, suppliers and service providers with an existing, active business relationship with MPMS, Inc.

Message content is limited to operational business communications, including:

  • Work-order assignment, dispatch, schedule changes and status updates
  • Purchase-order confirmations, material pickups and delivery coordination
  • Site-access and scheduling coordination
  • Urgent maintenance and property-condition notices
  • Internal operational alerts to MPMS staff

This program is not used for advertising, promotions, solicitation, sweepstakes, lead generation, or any other marketing purpose. It does not carry content related to cannabis, alcohol, firearms, tobacco or vaping, loans or lending, gambling, or any other restricted category.

2. How we obtain your consent

Consent to receive text messages from MPMS, Inc. is collected offline, in writing, during onboarding. We do not operate a public web sign-up form, a short-code keyword campaign, or any advertised call-to-action.

Vendors and contractors

When a vendor or contractor is set up as an approved MPMS, Inc. supplier, they complete our vendor onboarding paperwork. That paperwork asks for a contact mobile number and contains a written consent statement, signed by the vendor's authorized representative. The consent language reads:

By providing a mobile number above and signing below, you agree to receive operational SMS text messages from MPMS, Inc. regarding work orders, purchase orders, scheduling and deliveries. Consent is not a condition of doing business with MPMS, Inc. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See https://www.mpmsinc.com/messaging/ and https://www.mpmsinc.com/privacy/

See the actual vendor and employee consent forms.

A mobile number is enrolled only after that signed form is received. Providing a mobile number is optional, and consent to receive text messages is not a condition of being awarded work or doing business with MPMS, Inc. Vendors who decline are contacted by phone and email instead.

Employees

MPMS, Inc. employees provide a mobile number and give the same written consent as part of new-hire paperwork, on a form containing the identical disclosure language shown above. Employees may decline, or withdraw consent at any time, without affecting their employment.

Records

We retain the signed consent record for every enrolled mobile number for as long as the number remains enrolled and for at least four years afterward, and we can produce that record on request.

Every message we send identifies MPMS, Inc. by name. Representative examples:

MPMS, Inc.: Schedule change - your 9:00 AM Thursday appointment at Emerald Ridge has moved to 1:00 PM. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on operational activity — the number of work orders, purchase orders and schedule changes that involve you. Messages are sent as events occur rather than on a fixed schedule. A typical enrolled vendor or employee receives roughly 2 to 15 messages per week. There is no fixed maximum, because message volume follows real maintenance activity.

5. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. MPMS, Inc. does not charge you for these text messages, but your mobile carrier's standard message and data rates apply to every message you send or receive. Contact your wireless provider for details about your plan and any charges you may incur.

6. How to opt out

You may cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message you receive from MPMS, Inc. We also honor END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT and REVOKE.

After you send one of these keywords we will send a single confirmation message and then stop sending text messages to that number:

MPMS, Inc.: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no further texts from us. Reply START to resubscribe. Questions: (714) 238-1138

You may also opt out by emailing epost@mpmsinc.com or calling (714) 238-1138 and asking us to remove your number.

Opting out stops text messages only. MPMS, Inc. will continue to contact you about work orders, purchase orders and scheduling by phone and email. Opting out does not affect your business relationship or your employment with MPMS, Inc.

To resume text messages later, reply START (we also honor YES and UNSTOP) or contact us. You will receive one confirmation:

MPMS, Inc.: You are now opted in to MPMS Operations Alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Texting a keyword is the only way to resume messages after opting out. It is not a way to enroll in the first place — initial consent is always the signed form described in section 2.
